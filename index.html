BEFORE YOU SHARE

Verify first.
Amplify responsibly.

Viral posts can be misleading, outdated, or taken out of context. Use this quick checklist before treating a claim as fact.

01
Find the original source

Look for the original document, statement, image, or dataset.

02
Check date and context

Old footage and statistics can resurface with false captions.

03
Cross-check independently

Compare credible sources that do not simply repeat one another.

04
Label uncertainty

Separate confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unknowns.
