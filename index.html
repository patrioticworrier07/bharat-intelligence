06 / Analyst workflow

  1. Capture the exact claim, date, place and named actors.
  2. Find the earliest available primary statement or dataset.
  3. Seek two independent reputable reports where possible.
  4. Check publication time, updates, images and context.
  5. Separate confirmed facts, allegations, inference and unknowns.
  6. Record links and revisit if evidence changes.
